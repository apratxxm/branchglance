# Code of conduct

Branchglance is a small project, and everyone who takes part is expected to be respectful.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Keep feedback specific and useful, especially in reviews.
- Don't share other people's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/apratxxm/branchglance/security/advisories/new) and mention it is a conduct report. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who repeatedly break these rules.
